# Independent synthetic calculation appeals

An annual state-share calculation now continues from a member's appeal to an independently assigned adjudicator, a recorded hearing and a reasoned decision in the same record. The member can inspect the original amount, disputed and undisputed amounts, the reviewed amount, the difference and the recorded route for further review. The final result is a synthetic administrative calculation decision. It does not establish legal notice, make a court filing, stay collection, settle a transfer or issue a refund.

## Draft source and implementation boundary

The approved original documents remain unchanged and are identified by [the source manifest](../policy/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json).

| Draft source                                                                                     | Behavior implemented here                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Model State Act §5(f)](../policy/Model_State_Cornerstone_Enabling_Act.docx)                     | An independent administrative calculation appeal, a 60-day synthetic filing window, an explicit disputed amount and a reasoned affirm-or-correct decision. The original rate, method and compact remain fixed. The undisputed amount is displayed as due; no collection stay is inferred from a request.          |
| [Federal Act §6(d)](../policy/Federal_Cornerstone_Bill.docx) and Model State Act §5(a), (b), (f) | Review corrects inputs, arithmetic or classification under the published terms. It cannot substitute a negotiated percentage, create an allowance or renegotiate the compact.                                                                                                                                     |
| Model State Act §6(a)                                                                            | The owner, original calculating actors and original calculating institution cannot adjudicate the appeal. Current adjudicator role, privileged assurance, assignment, institution and absence of a record conflict are required. These technical exclusions do not certify an official's real-world independence. |
| Model State Act §7(a)                                                                            | The final decision includes a written further-review route. This simulation does not calculate a judicial deadline, validate venue, serve an order or lodge a case.                                                                                                                                               |

The draft's 60-day appeal language does not fully specify service mechanics. This synthetic implementation starts its calendar counter from an explicitly recorded **synthetic calculation notice date**. It accepts the final calendar date, including the end of that date in the simulation clock, and rejects the following date. The recorded date/reference are fixture evidence, not proof of legally sufficient service. Actual notice, tolling, a late-filing application and state-specific procedures need their own authorized implementation before use with real cases.

The separate 150-day annual schedule/payment period, final closing and supplemental settlements in §5(e), audited account reconciliation, and refund/credit election under lawful appropriation are outside this slice. A negative adjustment identifies a lower calculated amount; without confirmed payment history it does not establish an overpayment. The existing Treasury and settlement actions remain provider-gated.

## Usable sequence

1. An assigned fiscal actor calculates the submitted schedule or issues a transparent correction. The action records the synthetic notice date and reference, the compact and offer versions, enterprise, fiscal year, exact published terms and digests of the schedule and calculation. Corrections preserve the original compact, enterprise, fiscal year, rate and method.
2. The record owner files the appeal with reasons and an amount between zero and the original state share. Zero permits an appeal concerned with classification, including a zero calculation. The service recomputes and verifies the stored calculation before routing it. Missing independent routing or no eligible adjudicator produces a definitive prerequisite rejection, rolls back and leaves the calculated record unchanged.
3. The record moves to the separately configured adjudicator institution. It remains accessible to its owner and currently authorized reviewers. The adjudicator schedules a synthetic hearing. The member and assigned independent adjudicator can add and validate bounded synthetic text attachments while the appeal or scheduled hearing remains open.
4. On or after the scheduled date, the adjudicator records the synthetic hearing and selects validated supporting attachments. That action closes evidence preparation and freezes the selected IDs, plaintext hashes, text-check method/timestamps, a combined evidence digest and the exact hearing record version. The case-row lock serializes uploads, validation and hearing closure.
5. The independent adjudicator affirms the exact original inputs or submits corrected inputs in the existing guided cash editor. It begins with the original saved schedule. The service recomputes with the frozen original terms and verifies the closed evidence revision. The decision stores reasons, the further-review route, original/reviewed amounts, exact-cents adjustment and digests. The original calculation and hearing snapshot remain intact.

Every adjudicator action repeats owner, prior-calculator, institution, assignment, conflict and assurance checks. A narrow database predicate re-reads the current staff role as well, so a cached request actor cannot keep adjudicator powers after its role is removed while another role can still read. Review evidence writes use a corresponding current member-owner or independent-adjudicator predicate. Lists and downloads continue to use current row-level read scope and attachment integrity checks. No new attachment or validation can change a closed hearing or final decision.

## Recovery and verification

Calculation, appeal, hearing and decision receipts retain the existing idempotency and version checks. An uncertain browser response retains the exact original request. A retry returns the original historical receipt and record only while current read access remains available, then the workspace refreshes the current record. Concurrent identical decision requests produce one decision and one receipt. A rejected input, changed evidence revision or denied actor leaves the original calculation and record version unchanged.

The local unit suite covers fixed source versions and terms, exact-cents results, inclusive calendar boundaries, future notices, digest substitutions, invalid duplicated deductions, unchanged affirmation, mixed-role self-review, original-institution and prior-actor exclusions, missing assignments/conflicts and closed evidence stages. The PostgreSQL integration test exercises the actual transaction/routing functions, missing-route and no-reviewer rollback, fresh-role denial with a cached actor, historical and concurrent replay, immutable hearing evidence, failed decisions and access revocation. The desktop/mobile test follows the member and adjudicator interfaces, uploads evidence, closes the hearing, loses a committed decision response, retries the identical wire and verifies both original and reviewed amounts. It also checks accessibility and viewport overflow.

The fixtures explicitly seed downstream published-offer and effective-compact prerequisites. They do not attest enactment, legal authority, an exchange result or real compact execution. The sample fraction `1/20` and amounts are invented test data. Passing local unit/build checks is distinct from the required PostgreSQL and desktop/mobile CI acceptance. No independent human audit, legal activation or production readiness is claimed.
