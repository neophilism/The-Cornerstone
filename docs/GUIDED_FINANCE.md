# Guided financial preparation

State offers now collect the prospective Rate Act, published calculation version and exact rate fraction through labeled inputs. The rate has no default. One entry supplies the matching top-level references; the draft state's six-month working-capital, 36-month ordinary deadline and one-half productive-investment bounds are displayed as draft implementation choices. These bounds are not a state percentage or enacted law. A state must supply its prospective lawful rate through the prescribed public process.

Annual state-share schedules use guided receipt fields and itemized costs, prior losses and protected cash allowances. Money stays decimal text through exact-cents calculation. Source references, prior deductions, restricted cash, original deadlines and support for each amount remain explicit. Distributions and double deductions cannot reduce the share. A correction starts with the saved schedule and preserves the linked offer's rate and method.

The preparation schema excludes `terms`, `enterpriseId` and `year`. The API derives those from the compact, offer and fiscal-year field. A submission cannot introduce a privately selected rate. The calculation summary explains recognized amounts, protected Fund and debt payments, positive shareable surplus, the state land-account amount, allowed reserves and excluded costs. Zero shareable surplus means zero state payment. Calculation and submission receipts are never displayed as settled transfers.

The Founding Fund continues to use an itemized formation/feasibility/licensing/training budget, fixed public return and predetermined receipt fraction. Its enterprise-only debt protection remains required. Approval is not disbursement; Treasury confirmation remains disabled. The state surplus share is separate and conveys no equity, vote, board seat, veto or management right.

Verification includes a complete synthetic financial submission/calculation/correction/appeal journey and bounded Fund approval against PostgreSQL. Its prerequisite published offer and effective compact are explicitly invented database fixtures, not evidence of a completed legal exchange. The sample 1/20 state fraction and 1/10 Fund fraction are test data only. Expected ordered deductions are calculated independently: 1,000 receipts minus 100 operating costs, 100 prior losses, 100 Fund payments, 50 eligible debt principal and 100 working capital leaves 550, with a synthetic state share of 27.50. Duplicate-source corrections roll back; a zero-surplus correction yields no state payment. Desktop/mobile tests cover published terms, saved itemized cash inputs, assigned calculation and a member review request.

Real accounts, audit reconciliation, independent financial/security review, protected custody, settlement and legal activation remain prerequisites for production. This interface does not promise profitability or eliminate public fiscal risk.
